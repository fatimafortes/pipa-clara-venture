* Finding: One vecindad encargada said collecting "becomes a disaster because they don't pay on time." Neighbors also complained that some households pay late, but nobody admitted being one of them.
* Label: A. The encargada quote is n=1. The number of neighbors who complained isn't recorded.

2. The encargada paying "de su bolsa" is an assumption.

* Finding: The idea that the encargada sometimes covers other households with her own money is yours. No one has said it.
* Label: C, n=0.
* May claim: Only something conditional or a question: "¿Te ha tocado poner de tu bolsa?" or "Si alguna vez has puesto de tu bolsa…"
* May NOT claim: It as a fact about her or about encargadas in general ("ya no tendrás que poner de tu bolsa," "sabemos que siempre terminas pagando tú"). The headline "Ya no pongas de tu bolsa" rests on this assumption. As a statement it goes beyond your evidence, so it needs to be a question or a condition until someone actually confirms it.

3. Groups already wait for the full amount before ordering.

* Finding: The group votes on the deadline and sometimes moves the order back a day so no money is missing.
* Label: A. The number of groups or people who described this isn't recorded.
* May claim: That "the pipa is ordered only when 100% have paid" formalizes something groups already do sometimes. The message can present it as "lo que ya hacen, pero sin perseguir a nadie."
* May NOT claim:
   * That groups always wait, or that a one-day delay is acceptable to everyone.
   * That families are comfortable paying ahead of time. You haven't tested that.
   * That waiting for 100% is better than sometimes ordering an incomplete pipa. Only 3 of about 8 friends and family (week 13) said a non-payer is worse, and that group isn't vecindad residents.
