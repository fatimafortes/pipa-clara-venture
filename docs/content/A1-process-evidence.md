# A1 "¿Te ha tocado poner de tu bolsa?" — creative process evidence (Week 15)

Piece: WhatsApp invitation message for the encargada who organizes a shared private pipa, plus an untouched screenshot of www.pipa-clara.site/demo ("Juntando para la pipa").
Process: 6 roles, each a separate AI chat, each receiving only the previous role's output plus its rules. Founder approval at the end.
Date: 2026-11-22. Outputs below are copied verbatim from each chat.

---

## 01 · Research

**Input:** concept "Ya no pongas de tu bolsa" + product facts + founder evidence with A/C labels (A, n=1 encargada on late payments; A, neighbors' complaints; A, n=5 buy together; A, week 13 3 of ~8; A, n=1 pipero; A, group votes the deadline; C, encargada covers others; untested: paying in advance).
**Quality check:** every finding traceable to a person or labeled C.

**Output (verbatim):**

1. Late payment is the pain the encargada feels.

* Finding: One vecindad encargada said collecting "becomes a disaster because they don't pay on time." Neighbors also complained that some households pay late, but nobody admitted being one of them.
* Label: A. The encargada quote is n=1. The number of neighbors who complained isn't recorded.
* May claim: That collecting on time is hard. The message can say it in her words ("se vuelve un desastre") as a situation she might recognize.
* May NOT claim: That this is common ("a todas les pasa," "la mayoría"). It also can't say how often it happens or how much money is involved. And it can't say that hiding names ("5 de 6") will make late payers pay faster or feel comfortable. That link hasn't been tested.

2. The encargada paying "de su bolsa" is an assumption.

* Finding: The idea that the encargada sometimes covers other households with her own money is yours. No one has said it.
* Label: C, n=0.
* May claim: Only something conditional or a question: "¿Te ha tocado poner de tu bolsa?" or "Si alguna vez has puesto de tu bolsa…"
* May NOT claim: It as a fact about her or about encargadas in general ("ya no tendrás que poner de tu bolsa," "sabemos que siempre terminas pagando tú"). The headline "Ya no pongas de tu bolsa" rests on this assumption. As a statement it goes beyond your evidence, so it needs to be a question or a condition until someone actually confirms it.

3. Groups already wait for the full amount before ordering.

* Finding: The group votes on the deadline and sometimes moves the order back a day so no money is missing.
* Label: A. The number of groups or people who described this isn't recorded.
* May claim: That "the pipa is ordered only when 100% have paid" formalizes something groups already do sometimes. The message can present it as "lo que ya hacen, pero sin perseguir a nadie."
* May NOT claim:
   * That groups always wait, or that a one-day delay is acceptable to everyone.
   * That families are comfortable paying ahead of time. You haven't tested that.
   * That waiting for 100% is better than sometimes ordering an incomplete pipa. Only 3 of about 8 friends and family (week 13) said a non-payer is worse, and that group isn't vecindad residents.

**Founder note:** Research found that the planned headline "Ya no pongas de tu bolsa" rests on an assumption (C, n=0). It becomes a question.

## 03 · Visual

**Input:** the Writing message + source (screenshot of /demo, step "Juntando para la pipa", labeled "DEMO · datos de ejemplo") + visual system (teal #12707a, amber #b45f06, WhatsApp green #1f9150, ink #16272b, background #f2f6f7, Inter, large text, flat).
**Quality check:** real product, DEMO label visible, no names/faces/plates/homes, readable inside WhatsApp.

**Output (verbatim):**

I couldn't see the actual screenshot, so this spec assumes the usual layout for that step: a DEMO label, the step title, a progress block and a list of who has paid. Adjust it if the real screen is laid out differently.

1. What to capture and how to crop
Keep (top to bottom, in one frame):
* The "DEMO · datos de ejemplo" label in amber, uncut and with some space around it.
* The step title "Juntando para la pipa."
* The progress block: the count of who has paid (e.g. "9 de 12 ya dieron su parte"), the progress bar and the amount collected out of the total.
* The line or state that says the pipa gets ordered when everyone has paid, if it's on screen. That's the core promise of the message.
* The teal primary button, only if it sits right under the progress block. Otherwise leave it out.

Cut:
* The phone status bar (time, battery, carrier, notifications) and the browser URL bar. The link is already in the text.
* The list of who's missing, even with example names. The message says "el grupo, solo cuántos" ("the group only sees how many"). The image goes to the group, so it should show the count, not the names. This also keeps you safely inside the no-names rule.
* The keyboard, the bottom navigation and anything below the progress block.

Format: vertical 4:5 (1080 × 1350 px). WhatsApp crops very tall 9:16 images in the chat preview, so 4:5 shows whole without anyone tapping. Scale the crop so the smallest text left (probably the DEMO label) is at least ~28 px tall at 1080 wide. If the label ends up smaller, crop tighter rather than enlarging it artificially. If the crop doesn't fill 4:5, pad with the background color #f2f6f7, never with blur or a gradient.

2. Add anything? Nothing
* The text already explains the idea. A caption on the image would repeat it and make the image compete with the message.
* An untouched real screenshot is the proof that the product exists. Text, arrows or highlights would make it look like an ad or a mockup.
* The DEMO label already tells people the data is made up. A second warning would add noise.

The only allowed change is padding the margins with #f2f6f7. No frame, phone mockup or shadow.
Practical note: if you send the image with the message as its caption, WhatsApp won't show the link preview. That's fine, because the image already does that job and the URL stays clickable.

3. Verification checklist
1. DEMO and privacy: "DEMO · datos de ejemplo" is fully visible and readable. No name, phone number, face, plate or house appears anywhere, including small corners.
2. Phone readability: in a WhatsApp chat on a real phone, without zooming, you can read the title, the count ("X de Y") and the DEMO label. The image doesn't get cropped in the chat bubble.
3. One idea, real product: at a glance it says "we're collecting, X of Y are missing." It's an untouched screenshot, with no status bar, URL bar, keyboard or list of names.

**Founder note:** the screenshot itself is taken by the founder on a phone from production /demo, following this spec. Status: pending (taken before sending to the encargada).

## 05 · Style review

**Input:** the piece + voice rules + visual rules (facts already checked).
**Quality check:** consistent voice and look.

**Output (verbatim):**

Style issues (4) + word count

1. The three opening questions ("¿tú organizas…? … ¿se vuelve un desastre? … ¿Te ha tocado poner de tu bolsa?")
Three questions in a row sound like a sales pitch that pokes at a pain point, which is company voice. "Desastre" also adds drama. Keep one or two at most, for example: "¿Tú organizas la pipa? ¿Te ha tocado poner de tu bolsa?" That also frees up words for the rewrite.
2. "de la vecindad"
In CDMX, "vecindad" usually means one specific kind of housing, so it may not match the reader's building. Use whatever they'd call it themselves: "del edificio", "de la unidad" or "de la cuadra".
3. Sentence 4, tone only ("Pipa Clara hace lo que a veces ya hacen…")
The brand is the subject, which sounds like a company talking. "Hacen" (ustedes) also breaks the "tú" used everywhere else. Have the neighbor talk and stay with "tú", for example: "Con Pipa Clara pides la pipa cuando…" (claim wording to follow the fact fix). Keep "sin perseguir a nadie", it's good.
4. "Tú ves quién falta; el grupo, solo cuántos."
The semicolon and the dropped verb read like written prose, not WhatsApp. Split it into two short sentences: "Tú ves quién falta. El grupo solo ve cuántos." The privacy point (rule 3) stays as it is.
5. Word count (rule 4)
The message is at 58 words plus the link, so there's almost no room left. The sentence 4 rewrite has to come in at the same length or shorter, unless you cut a question as in item 1.

Visual: none. It's an untouched real screenshot with the DEMO label, one idea, and it shows only the count, so it meets the rules. I didn't check colors or font because it's an unedited screenshot.
No pressure language and no blame on the pipero, so rules 2 and 3 pass.

**Conflict:** Style review said to keep "sin perseguir a nadie"; Claim check failed it. Rule set by the founder: **Claim check wins (facts before style).**

## 06 · Revision

**Input:** current piece + Claim check fails + Style issues + the conflict rule + product facts.
**Quality check:** every fail and issue closed.

**Output (verbatim):**

1. Mensaje final
"Hola, ¿tú organizas la pipa del edificio o la vecindad? ¿Te ha tocado poner de tu bolsa cuando alguien paga tarde? Con Pipa Clara, tú pides la pipa cuando todos dieron su parte: la app anota quién pagó. Tú ves quién falta. El grupo solo ve cuántos. Cobras en efectivo, como siempre. Míralo: www.pipa-clara.site/demo"

2. Conteo: 53 palabras, más el link.

3. Cambios
Claim check
* "lo que a veces ya hacen": Lo quité. Ya no se dice nada sobre lo que la gente hace hoy.
* "sin perseguir a nadie": Lo quité y puse lo que la app sí hace: "la app anota quién pagó" y "Tú ves quién falta". "Cobras en efectivo, como siempre" deja claro que ella sigue cobrando en persona.

Estilo
1. Tres preguntas y "desastre": Ahora son dos preguntas. Quité la del "desastre" y metí "cuando alguien paga tarde" en la de "poner de tu bolsa".
2. "vecindad": Ahora dice "del edificio o la vecindad", para que funcione en los dos casos.
3. La marca como sujeto en la oración 4: El sujeto ahora es "tú pides la pipa". Pipa Clara solo aparece como complemento ("Con Pipa Clara"). Ya no dice "hacen".
4. Oración larga: La partí en dos: "Tú ves quién falta. El grupo solo ve cuántos."
5. Límite de 60 palabras: Quedó en 53.

Conflicto
* "sin perseguir a nadie": Estilo quería conservarlo, pero Claim check lo marcó como falla, así que se quitó porque Claim check tiene prioridad.
